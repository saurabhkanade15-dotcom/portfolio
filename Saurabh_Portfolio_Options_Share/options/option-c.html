About

I make visuals that stop the scroll and edits that keep people watching.

I am a Visual Designer and Video Editor from Pune with over five years in the creative industry. My work runs across graphic and visual design, branding and identity, video editing and motion graphics, UI/UX and product design, illustration, production shoots and filmmaking, and increasingly AI-assisted creative production. Today I am Senior Visual Designer at SRV Media, where I create and execute advertising creatives and lead a small team of designers.

  • Task allocationDaily planning and guidance for the designers I mentor.
  • Creative reviewFeedback on concept, craft and brand fit before anything reaches the client.
  • Client requirementsTurning what the client says into what the creative needs to do.
  • Account coordinationWorking with Account Managers on scope, timelines and delivery.
  • Project ownershipQuality and on-time delivery are mine, from first idea to final file.
  • MentorshipSharing process, tools and AI workflows so the team levels up together.

Concept first, then craft.

Every creative starts with the idea that will make someone stop. The design, the edit and the motion exist to deliver that idea, not to decorate it.

Performance is part of the brief.

I design for ads that have to work: clear hierarchy, one message, a reason to act. Pretty is the floor, not the goal.

AI speeds up the hands, not the thinking.

I use AI for generation, editing, mockups and video so the team spends its time on judgement, review and quality.
