How I work

Three things I never skip.

Concept first, then craft.

Every creative starts with the idea that will make someone stop. The design, the edit and the motion exist to deliver that idea, not to decorate it.

Performance is part of the brief.

I design for ads that have to work: clear hierarchy, one message, a reason to act. Pretty is the floor, not the goal.

AI speeds up the hands, not the thinking.

I use AI for generation, editing, mockups and video so the team spends its time on judgement, review and quality.

Leading the team

Owning the work, not just the file.

At SRV Media I oversee two designers alongside my own creative work: allocating tasks, translating client requirements into briefs, reviewing every piece before it ships, and representing the creative team in client and account meetings.

Task allocation

Daily planning and guidance for the designers I mentor.

Creative review

Feedback on concept, craft and brand fit before anything reaches the client.

Turning what the client says into what the creative needs to do.

Project ownership

Quality and on-time delivery are mine, from first idea to final file.

Mentorship
