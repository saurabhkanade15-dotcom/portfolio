Case study 06 · Healthcare

An ambulance management system for a hospital

A booking and fleet-management system with admin, driver and patient modules plus reporting and analytics, designed for Onkar Hospital in Supa.

HealthcareOnkar Hospital, Supa
Role
Product and UI/UX design
Client
Healthcare
Disciplines
  • UI/UX
  • Product Design
  • Figma
  • Web

The brief

Design a system that lets patients book an ambulance, lets the hospital add and manage vehicles, generates reports, and captures feedback and complaints.

The challenge

Three very different users, one system. A patient in an emergency, a driver on the road and an administrator at a desk need different screens that stay consistent with each other.

The approach

  • Mapped the four core features (book ambulance, add ambulance, report generation, feedback and complaints) to the people who use them.
  • Designed four modules: Admin, Driver, Patient, and Reporting & Analytics.
